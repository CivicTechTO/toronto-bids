# Procurement archive language

## Bidding

**Participation**:
Published evidence that a supplier submitted an offer to a procurement, including an offer subsequently rejected. One supplier can participate through several scoped or alternative submissions.

**Bid scope**:
A source-labelled lot, part, category, subcategory or region to which an offer or outcome applies. An unstated scope remains unstated.

**Submission**:
An actual offer for a bid scope, possibly one of several alternatives from the same supplier. A submission is distinct from a firm and from its price variants.

**Response observation**:
A source record of a submission, an explicit decision not to bid, or an outcome whose submission scope is unstated. Observing a “No Bid” cell does not establish a submission.

**Price variant**:
A quoted value with its source basis, unit and qualifications, such as a one-year total, an all-option-years total, or a percentage discount from a stated list price. Variants are not separate submissions.
