# Bulk archived City award recovery — 2026-11-09

**380 previously uncovered awarded City solicitations now export with named bids in the production-copy rehearsal.** Missing coverage falls from **3,686 to 3,306**, against the frozen November 9 baseline of 6,451 awarded solicitations. This exceeds the 340-record acceptance threshold; it is one combined workload on PR #300, not another standalone small recovery.

The newly found source is the original City competitive award portal, retained by the Internet Archive. Nine populated original views contain native Call Numbers, full suppliers, exact HST-exclusive contract amounts and Date Awarded fields. The City's own original heading supplies the tax role. The source supplies award identity evidence, not additional losing-bid names: the named bid observations already existed in the archive and lacked independently established City-spine attribution.

The complete fresh publisher capture contains **7,817 records**, including repeated and junk document keys. All remain in the ambiguity comparison; repeated target keys are refused. Original portal amount, full supplier and printed award date must match exactly and uniquely across the full frozen publisher population, the held historical portal views and current held facts. Native calls must independently match a single original agenda title and labeled Call Number field. Numerical call encodings, rounded amounts and fuzzy supplier names play no part in admission.

The archive pass adds **346** reviewed source proofs. Together with the **34** earlier branch recoveries, the measured workload covers **380** distinct additional solicitations. Eighteen archive candidates overlap the earlier 34 and are not counted twice. Two otherwise qualified candidates with repeated publisher keys remain held. Nine source snapshot rows with award dates after their archive capture were excluded; these are not silently corrected.

Existing counterevidence is preserved. The independent review identified different council initial amounts, source supplier spellings, methods and source dates; the new proof does not claim these agree. Exact portal supplier/award facts identify the event, while raw council bidder names, prices and stage/lot observations remain unchanged. No archive winner row is copied into a losing-bid or bid-price field. See [independent primary-source review](city-archived-award-evidence-review-2026-11-09.md).

The earlier complete-agenda acquisition and historical extraction backlog were already processed. The whole-population census did not find hundreds of unprocessed held reports: only four historical hypotheses lacked current caches. A 24-target stratified attachment-search pilot and a known-positive all-term control were retained as discovery evidence, not recoveries; large search-result counts require paging and exact source-context review. The tiny City MERX mirror exposed QA test suppliers and was excluded.
