# Research: 22 City identity holds outside the compound-facts group — November 5, 2026

This review covers every frozen identity-unresolved case whose initial refusal was not `compound facts do not identify one global publisher record`: 12 type refusals, six date refusals and four call/title refusals. It reads held original agenda HTML, held report text/PDF metadata and the original November 4 OData response. It changes no runtime code, registry, cache or production data and makes no model/network requests.

The baseline reproducible command is `/home/alex/toronto-bids/scrapers/.venv/bin/python .validation-city-identity-holds-2026-11-05/research-other.py`; it drives the actual `city_crosswalk.propose` gate and records the refusal for each original source context. The duplicate-context case passes proposal generation but is refused by the separate global council-context gate, as expected.

## Findings

- Two cases have strong safe recovery paths: UBA `6606137147` through recognizing the literal “Request of Quotation” spelling, and `9117167066` through an explicitly labelled native call plus either original publisher native field or exact multiwinner corroboration.
- Seven additional identities have a source/publisher type contradiction but exact two source dates, a full supplier identity and initial net award that identify exactly one record across **all** publisher types. They require an explicit reviewed discrepancy proof preserving both original types; a generic type-ignore rule is unsupported. One of those seven, SCI Interiors, still has bid submission/category scope requiring a hold.
- The other apparent syntax fixes expose additional supplier/date/lot issues; they do not justify recoveries on their own.
