## Coverage audit

[November 23 public SQLite release](https://github.com/CivicTechTO/toronto-bids-data/releases/tag/latest).

## Routine disclosure for the three modern holds

Targeted exact-Doc and title searches covered the City’s award attachments, council records, and published files. Exact-Doc checks of the City’s OData and Open Data award records yielded only award or pre-submission material. The City’s post-Bid Award Panel transparency report explains that delegated open awards are intended to carry an Award Summary Form listing bidders. The shelter and organic-facilities records reviewed above did not supply that form. [Procurement transparency report, p. 5](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2025/gg/bgrd/backgroundfile-256906.pdf#page=5), [City TOBids Awarded Contracts dataset](https://open.toronto.ca/dataset/tobids-awarded-contracts/).

The City’s current access directory links to the Purchasing & Materials Management Division Routine Disclosure Plan. Its solicitation-results procedure routes email requests to the responsible buyer via `citybids@toronto.ca` and provides supplier names at closing for RFPs and RFSQs. The Toronto Bids Portal also directs public inquiries to the posted buyer. If the division cannot supply the records through routine disclosure, the City provides a formal Access and Privacy Unit request process. [City access directory](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/access-city-information-or-records/how-to-access-city-information/), [PMMD Routine Disclosure Plan, p. 5](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/9717-PMMD-Routine-Disclosure-Plan.pdf#page=5), [Toronto Bids Portal](https://www.toronto.ca/business-economy/doing-business-with-the-city/searching-bidding-on-city-contracts/toronto-bids-portal/), [City FOI process](https://www.toronto.ca/city-government/accountability-operations-customer-service/access-city-information-or-records/freedom-of-information/).
