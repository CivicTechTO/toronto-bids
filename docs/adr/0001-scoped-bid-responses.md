# Preserve scoped responses separately from legacy bid observations

Keep existing bid rows and their archive IDs unchanged, and store source-labelled bid scopes, response observations and price variants separately. For newly reviewed scoped reports, publish an unpriced participation summary per proven supplier alongside the detailed responses; count firms, submissions and source cells separately. Widening the legacy price-based bid key would leave scopes implicit and alter the meaning of historical counts, while treating every lot or price column as another firm would overstate competition.

Explicit “No Bid” cells, unidentified source fragments, unscoped outcomes and cancelled categories remain visible with their own meanings. A scoped price or outcome never becomes a procurement-wide claim, and a percentage or unit rate never becomes a currency total.
