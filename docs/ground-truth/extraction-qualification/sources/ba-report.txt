SUMMARY
The purpose of this report is to rescind the Bid Award Panel award of Request for
Quotation (RFQ) 4201-18-5067 as adopted by the Bid Award Panel on November 28,
2018, BA110.4
(http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2018.BA110.4). BA110.4
was submitted in error and RFQ 4201-18-5067 was previously awarded by the Bid
Award Panel on October 31, 2018, BA104.3
(http://app.toronto.ca/tmmis/viewAgendaItemHistory.do?item=2018.BA104.3).

RECOMMENDATIONS
The Chief Purchasing Officer recommends that the Bid Award Panel:
1. Rescind its November 28, 2018 decision to award Request for Quotation No. 420118-5067 as per item BA110.4, as this report was submitted in error.

FINANCIAL IMPACT
There are no financial impact arising from the recommendation in this report. The
financial impacts are set out in BA104.3.
