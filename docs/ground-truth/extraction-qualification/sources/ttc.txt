Purchase Of Brake Drums
Meeting Date: December 13, 2000

Subject: Purchase Of Brake Drums

Recommendation

It is recommended that the Commission authorize the awarding of a three year inventory supply
contract for brake drums to Neopart, and MCI Service Parts Co. with a combined upset limit of
$261,000.00 including applicable taxes for the period from January 2001 to December 2003.

Funding

Sufficient funds will be included in the 2001 TTC Operating Budget and will be included in
future Operating Budgets as appropriate. These inventory materials will be charged to the
appropriate budget at the time of usage.

Background

The subject brake drums are used on the Commission’s bus fleet. Staff have estimated that over
the three year period, approximately 2,500 pieces of 2 part types will be required to support
maintenance needs.

Discussion

Tenders were invited from thirteen known suppliers coupled with an advertisement in the Globe
and Mail on November 7, 2000. Six companies submitted tenders as summarized in Appendix
‘A’.

Neopart is the lowest acceptable tenderer on item #1 and MCI Services Part Co. is the lowest
acceptable tenderer on item #2. Neither of the companies included exceptions or qualification in
their tenders and both are considered acceptable.

Initially, Neopart will be allocated $229,000.00 and MCI Services Part Co. will be allocated
$32,000.00. During the terms of these contracts, adjustments to the allocations may be made
based on the actual part usage.

The pricing for the new three year contract is approximately 1% lower over the three years of the
contract than the previous pricing under the current three year contract.

Justification
The brake drums are required in order to support maintenance needs.
