RECOMMENDATIONS

The Director of Purchasing and Materials Management recommends that the Bid
Committee grant authority to award the following contract:

Call No:                 RFP No. 9121-12-7092
Description:             To provide professional services to undertake the Richmond –
                         Adelaide Corridor Cycle Tracks Planning and Design Study to
                         evaluate and recommend the preferred alignment and produce a
                         preliminary design for physically separated bicycle lanes (cycle
                         tracks) in the Richmond-Adelaide Corridor, from Bathurst
                         Street to Sherbourne Street. Based on an expected
                         commencement in early January 2013, the Contract should be
                         completed by the end of December 2013.
