SUMMARY

The purpose of this report is to advise of the results of the Request for Proposal (RFP)
Doc4929553192, Contract Number RFP-24ECS-BE-01PM, for Professional Consulting
Services for the Bridge Program Management Assignment No. 3, and to request the
authority to enter into an agreement with Stantec Consulting Ltd., in the amount of
$20,427,325 including contingency and net of all applicable taxes and charges
($20,786,846 net of HST recoveries).

This work is an essential part of the City's broader Bridge Rehabilitation Program, which
ensures Toronto’s bridges and culverts are maintained in a state of good repair. These
investments help prolong the life of vital infrastructure, enhance public safety, and
reduce long-term capital costs.

The Bridge Rehabilitation Program supports the state-of-good-repair of City-owned
bridges and culverts. This Assignment will provide full consulting services—including
program management, design, and construction supervision—for the rehabilitation of 28
bridges between 2025 and 2028.

Stantec Consulting Ltd., was selected as the highest-scoring proponent based on
technical and financial evaluations, demonstrating both strong qualifications and
competitive pricing. The procurement followed a transparent two-envelope process with
a rigorous technical threshold.

COMMENTS


Background
The Bridge Rehabilitation Program is funded through Transportation Services’ Capital
Budget. Its program’s goal is to maintain the City's bridges and culverts in a state of
good repair. Projects are identified through the City's biannual bridge inspections and
the scope of work typically includes structural concrete repairs, removal and
replacement of deteriorated components, cleaning and coating of steel girders, bearing
replacements, and removal and replacement of barrier walls.

This Bridge Program Management Assignment No. 3 will provide program
management, data collection, preliminary design, detailed design, services during
construction, and post-construction services to deliver a portion of the annual Bridge
Rehabilitation Program designated for construction from 2026 to 2028. The program
includes the rehabilitation of 28 bridges. Design services are scheduled to commence in
2025, with construction activities beginning in 2026 and anticipated to be completed by
the end of 2028. Post-construction services will be provided for a period of two years
following the completion of construction.

Procurement Process
Request for Proposal Doc4929553192, Contract Number RFP-24ECS-BE-01PM, was
prepared by Engineering and Construction Services in consultation with Transportation
Services, Legal Services, and Purchasing and Materials Management, to select a
qualified Supplier for the project. The Request for Proposal was advertised on both the
City’s and Ariba Discovery websites on January 10, 2025, with a closing date of
February 28, 2025, and the issuance of eight addenda. This resulted in a total of five
submissions received on February 28, 2025, from the following Suppliers:

1. EXP Services Inc.*
2. Hatch Ltd.
3. Parsons Inc.*
4. Stantec Consulting Ltd.
5. WSP Canada Inc.
*Supplier did not meet the minimum technical threshold
 Range of scores: 91.4 to 84.3

The Request for Proposals was structured as a "two-envelope" submission model that
included a detailed Technical Proposal (Envelope 1) and a separate sealed envelope
with a Cost of Services Proposal (Envelope 2). The selection process stipulated that the
envelopes containing the Cost of Services Proposals would not be opened until the
evaluation of the Technical Proposals had been completed.

A formal selection committee comprised of three members from the Engineering and
Construction Services staff evaluated the five Technical Proposals based on the criteria
set out in the Request for Proposal. A maximum of 80 points were allocated for the
Technical Proposal and 20 points for the Cost of Services Proposal. A minimum
threshold technical score of 75% (60 out of 80 points) was required to proceed in having
the Cost of Services Proposal envelope opened and evaluated.

All Suppliers complied with the mandatory submission requirements and their Technical
Proposals were evaluated. Of the five submissions, the selection committee found two
of the five suppliers did not meet the minimum technical threshold and three Suppliers
successfully passed the Technical Proposal requirements which allowed their Cost of
Services Proposal envelope to be opened. The cost proposal scores were added to the
technical proposal scores which resulted in overall scores ranging from 91.2 to 84.3 out
of 100 total points (84% to 91%).

On completion of the above process, the selection committee concluded that the
proposal submitted by Stantec Consulting Ltd., met the requirements of
the Request for Proposal. The Supplier's scores and staff analysis of the evaluation
results can be provided to Councillors in an in-camera presentation if requested by
members of Council.

Two Purchase Orders will be issued for Request for Proposal Doc4929553192 for
Professional Consulting Services for the Bridge Program Management Assignment
No. 3 to Stantec Consulting Ltd. The first Purchase Order will be issued to cover
program management, data collection, preliminary design, detailed design, and services
during construction in the amount of $20,155,006 including contingency and net of all
applicable taxes and charges ($20,509,734 net of HST recoveries). The second
Purchase Order will be issued to cover post construction services in the amount of
$272,319 including contingency and net of all applicable taxes and charges ($277,111
net of HST recoveries).

The Fair Wage Office has reported that the recommended Supplier has indicated it has
reviewed and understands the Fair Wage Policy and Labour Trades requirements and
has agreed to comply fully with both.
