What kind of document is this?

125 documents drawn from every source the archive collects — Conservation Authority board reports, Exhibition Place, the Toronto Zoo, City council reports, Award Summary Forms and committee reports — shuffled together. The source isn't shown, because knowing it gives away the likely answer.

The archive only wants procurement: bids, awards, suppliers. Everything else gets classified and then deliberately ignored — so the point of this exercise is to find out what's actually in the pile, including the categories nobody has thought of yet.

Pick a category with a number key or a click. If nothing fits, use Other and type what it actually is — a category we're missing is the most useful thing you can give us here.

There's also a checkbox for "this contains bid or award information". That one matters independently: it tells us whether a document we'd otherwise skip is hiding real procurement data.

About ten seconds each. Progress saves automatically; you can stop whenever and come back.
