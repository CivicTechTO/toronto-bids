## Scope and rate limits

- **Included:** Anonymous public Open and Past listing metadata (3 serial GETs per buyer: landing page, Open collection, Past collection; no per-project requests). Attribution credits each publisher and retains public opportunity URLs (the shared TCHC/TSHC portal credits both corporations).
- **Excluded:** Bid documents, private/invited projects, bidder submissions, logins, registration, and platform-wide crawling.
- **Rate limits:** At least 1s between requests, max 3 retries with exponential backoff on transport/429/5xx errors, honoring `Retry-After` and persisting >60s deferrals across runs.
