# TRCA: permission to archive public bid listings

- **Body:** Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA)
- **Portal:** `https://trca.bidsandtenders.ca/`
- **Granted:** 2026-07-18, by the TRCA Procurement Department (`procurement@trca.ca`)
- **Enabled in:** `config.BIDS_TENDERS_PORTALS["trca"]`

## Scope and conditions

Covers periodic, read-only, rate-limited fetching of publicly visible listing metadata on `trca.bidsandtenders.ca` (solicitation number, title, status, posted/closing dates) for the Toronto Bids public archive. Excludes logging in, downloading bid documents, and anything behind a vendor account.

Conditions:

- **Rate limiting:** Keep nightly fetches low-impact so they do not trigger firewall blocks.
- **Attribution:** Credit TRCA as the data source in published exports.
- **Point of contact:** `procurement@trca.ca` for technical issues or unexpected blocks.

Request sent: [`docs/letters/2026-07-18-trca-portal-permission.md`](../letters/2026-07-18-trca-portal-permission.md).
