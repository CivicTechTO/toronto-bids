# MERX public-data reproduction authorization

On 2026-10-24, in the MERX implementation conversation, the project maintainer
confirmed: “I have received authorization to reproduce this data in the context of
open data.” This records the authorization supplied for MERX data in the Toronto
Bids open-data archive. The grantor, instrument and additional conditions were not
specified in the conversation; this record does not invent them or claim an
independent review of a permission document.

This resolves the project-specific reproduction question raised by #133 and the
[MERX reconnaissance](../merx-recon-2026-10-24.md). The authorization is supplied by
the maintainer; it is not inferred from public accessibility, robots directives,
a supplier subscription or the separate Bonfire permission.

The planned capture remains scoped to the identified buyers' public notices,
published bid results and award information, with source URLs and attribution
retained. It uses an identifying archive User-Agent, serial requests at least five
seconds apart, bounded backoff and server Retry-After deadlines. Authentication,
registration, supplier submissions, private documents, challenge bypass and
unbounded platform-wide crawling are outside this capture scope.

Record incomplete historical coverage explicitly until the observed pagination
limit is resolved. Authorization to reproduce the data does not establish that
all historical records are technically accessible, nor turn unevaluated bidder
results into evaluated bids or completed awards.
