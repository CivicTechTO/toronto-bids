## Limits and follow-up

The 964 disclosure/revision rows lack public-call identifiers in the held schemas. That is a limitation of these documents, not proof that the originating procurements can never be identified. Their original procurement or carried award sources are still needed; revision dates and original costs must not be turned into new solicitation or award facts.

The remaining 49 contexts also need specific primary-source follow-up. Cooperative OECM arrangements need the applicable host/local call binding, not a VOR label; developer exchanges and negotiated financial/parking services may have no public call; construction-manager subcontract awards must not be represented as direct TRCA procurement decisions. A resolution or funding account is not a solicitation identifier.

The 2023 integrated-design extension explicitly cites A156/17, so its parent RFP10004424 is now resolvable, but the $5.5m value is a revised cumulative agreement ceiling and remains held as a revision. Other concrete defects include study budgets combining staff and consultant costs, aggregate VOR ceilings repeated for every supplier, the audit source’s omitted Deloitte quote and Ernst&Young decline, a Cudmore supplier-name conflation, and parking decisions referred back rather than approved on proposed five-year terms. Identity recovery must not release those incorrect roles.
